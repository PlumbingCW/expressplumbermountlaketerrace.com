Overflowing floor drain or restroom flood right now

A flooded floor in a business creates a slip risk and can wreck stock. These steps buy you time.

  • 1Stop water going into the drain. Turn off sinks, dishwashers and ice machines that feed it.
  • 2Keep staff and customers out of the wet area, and put out signs.
  • 3Shut the nearest fixture valves. If the flood continues, close the main shutoff.
  • 4Lift stock, cords and paper off the floor if it is dry to do so.

Buildings with many fixtures behave differently

A public building like the Recreation Pavilion, with several pools, locker rooms and an indoor gym, shows how many fixtures one roof can hold. We do not claim to serve it. We use it to make a point about any large building. The more fixtures on a line, the more important a shutoff map becomes.

If you manage a space like that, find out where each zone can be shut off before something breaks. A plumber who can isolate one restroom is faster than one who has to close the whole building. Keep that list at the front desk.

Apartments, multi-unit buildings and the Town Center blocks

Town Center, between 220th and 236th Streets, has new apartment and mixed-use buildings, and those combine homes, shops and shared mechanical rooms in one structure. A leak in one unit can reach several units below it.

If you manage a building like that, the first question is whether the problem sits inside a unit or on a shared riser or stack. Tenants need a clear way to report water, and managers need a plumber who can talk to both. Water surfacing in a parking lot or a toilet backing up in one unit are typical examples.

What a facility manager can do before we arrive

A few steps cut the damage and speed up the job.

  • ✓Know your shutoffs. Find the main and the zone valves, and mark them.
  • ✓Keep a drain map. Note which fixtures feed which floor drain.
  • ✓Do not pry grates by hand. Steel covers are heavy and can crush fingers.
  • ✓Stay out of electrical rooms. Standing water near panels is a shock hazard, so call the utility or 911 if there is sparking.
  • ✓Keep an access contact. A person with keys for roof, boiler and utility rooms saves time.
