A big neighborhood with more than one kind of street

City planners treat Cascade View as one of the six sub-areas of Mountlake Terrace. They also describe it as large and varied, and commissioners have raised whether it may be better split into smaller areas. Terrain came up in that boundary discussion.

For you, that means addresses differ a lot from block to block. When you call (206) 864-6558, give your street and a cross street, and say if you rent or own. It saves a second call.

Slopes, pressure and where water wants to go

Ground that slopes changes plumbing in two ways. Water supply pressure tends to run higher at fixtures on low ground, and drains rely on a steady fall to move waste.

A pressure reducing valve near the main shutoff keeps pressure in a safe range. When it fails, faucets spray, pipes knock and a water heater's relief valve may drip. A plumber can read a pressure gauge at a hose bib in a few minutes.
