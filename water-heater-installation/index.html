Water Heater Installation in Mountlake Terrace, WA

Buying the tank is the easy part. Water heater installation in Mountlake Terrace, WA means getting the venting, fuel, electrical, relief valve and drain right so the unit is safe on day one.

A water heater is not a swap of two hoses

People are sometimes surprised by how much surrounds a water heater. The tank sits between five connections: cold supply, hot outlet, fuel or power, exhaust, and the relief valve discharge. A mistake at any of them can mean scalding, leaks, carbon monoxide or a flooded closet.

Our job is to make each connection correct and to make sure the new unit suits the space. We also run a 24/7 line, so if something looks wrong after the install, a dispatcher picks up.

What a proper installation includes

These are the connections and parts we set up on a new tank.

  • ✓Shutoff valve. A full-port valve on the cold supply so the tank can be isolated for service.
  • ✓Flexible or rigid connectors. Dielectric fittings where dissimilar metals meet, to slow corrosion.
  • ✓T&P valve and discharge pipe. Piped to a safe termination, never capped or reduced.
  • ✓Expansion tank. Added when the system is closed and the tank needs it.
  • ✓Venting or electrical. Flue and draft hood, power-vent pipe or a correctly sized circuit.
  • ✓Drain pan. Recommended where a leak would damage floors, finishes or rooms below.
  • ✓Strapping. Wall straps or a seismic kit hold the tank upright.

What drives the cost of a water heater installation

Installation price depends on the site more than on the tank. These factors apply.

Unit type

Standard tank, power-vent, direct-vent and heat pump each need different parts and labor.

Venting changes

Moving from a chimney to a side wall means new pipe, new clearances and a wall penetration.

Fuel conversion

Gas to electric or heat pump brings circuit work, capping the gas and sometimes a condensate drain.

Supply condition

Corroded connectors, old shutoffs and aged piping near the tank often need replacing at the same time.

Supply-and-install or labor only

Who buys the unit changes the scope, and the warranty on the unit stays between you and its maker.

Code and permit questions in Washington

Washington adopts the 2021 edition of the Uniform Plumbing Code, published by IAPMO, with state amendments. That is the framework behind questions on relief valves, discharge piping and pressure. It does not tell you everything about your house, so we do not guess about specific sections.

For a permit question, call the City of Mountlake Terrace permit center before the work starts. The city lists plumbing, mechanical and electrical permit types, and permit rules vary with the scope of the job. You can also check any contractor you plan to hire on the state L&I Verify tool, which shows registration status and violations.

Safety around gas and electric connections

If you smell gas near a gas heater, leave the building and call the gas utility or 911 first, then call us. We do not service gas lines, and no pilot should be relit when there is a smell.

On electric units, never open a live panel to work on the circuit yourself. If a breaker trips again and again, leave it off and call. For other jobs near the heater, see our plumbing installation page.
