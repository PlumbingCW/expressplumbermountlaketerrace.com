Rain, a lake and a leak: how to tell which one you have

Lake Ballinger is a lake of roughly 100 acres in the southwest part of Mountlake Terrace. Hall Creek feeds it from Lynnwood and McAleer Creek carries water out toward Lake Washington. Homes near any lake or creek can see damp ground in the wet season, so a wet spot does not always mean a broken pipe.

The meter test settles it. Turn off every fixture, note the dial, and check again in an hour. If it moved, water is escaping from a pipe and you should call (206) 864-6558. If it held still, the moisture is likely coming from outside the plumbing.

Yards near Ballinger Park and what roots do to sewer lines

Ballinger Park covers 42 acres at the north end of the lake. It was a golf course until the city converted it to a park in 2013.

Roots look for moisture, and a sewer lateral joint can leak a little. If your yard has large trees near the line and drains slow down every few months, ask for a camera inspection before paying for repeated clearing. A camera on a flexible cable shows whether roots, a sag or a crack is the cause, and that decides between drain cleaning and repair.

After a heavy storm: five minutes that save a bad guess

  • ✓Find the cleanout. Know where the sewer cleanout cap sits and keep the area clear. Do not open it if water is rising.
  • ✓Find the main shutoff. Test the handle once so you know it turns before you need it.
  • ✓Watch the lowest drain. A basement or ground-floor drain that burbles first points to a main line problem.
  • ✓Keep wet electrical away. Stay clear of outlets, panels and appliances that are wet.
  • ✓Call early. A slow drain is easier to fix than a backup that has already reached the floor.

Houses, apartments and who to call first

The area around the lake is residential and includes detached houses as well as apartment buildings. In a house, the drain, water heater and supply lines are yours to fix. In an apartment, a shared drain stack or a building water heater is usually the landlord's.

Tell the property manager before booking anything. If the cause turns out to be a fixture inside your unit, our faucet repair and toilet repair calls are quick to scope. For the wider picture, see our Mountlake Terrace service area page or the neighboring Lynnwood page.
