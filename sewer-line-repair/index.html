Repair does not have to mean a trench across the yard

If sewage is backing up or a sewer smell is filling the house, call now. Our line is open 24/7, and while you wait you can stop water use and keep people out of the affected rooms.

Many people assume that a broken sewer pipe means the yard gets torn up from house to street. Sometimes it does. These are the general options for a damaged sewer lateral. Which one applies depends on the pipe and the layout of your property.

Signs the pipe is damaged, not just clogged

A clog clears. A damaged pipe keeps coming back. These signs point to damage.

  • ✓Repeat backups. The line is fine after cleaning, then blocked again in weeks or months.
  • ✓Slow drains throughout the house. A sag or partial collapse limits the flow.
  • ✓Soggy or sunken yard spots. Waste water may be leaking into the soil.
  • ✓Sewer odor outdoors or in the house. A break can let gas out near the foundation.
  • ✓Roots on every cable pass. Roots need a gap to enter.
  • ✓Gurgling or a P-trap that will not hold water. A vent or line problem pulls the water out.

What changes the price of a sewer line repair

Sewer repair ranges from a small fix to a major project, so the factors matter.

Length of pipe involved

A single joint is a different job from a long stretch of lateral.

Depth and location

Pipe under a driveway, patio or tree needs more work than a clear lawn.

Repair method

Spot repair, lining and replacement each have different labor and materials.

Pipe material and condition

Failing old pipe may need more replaced than the visible damage.

Permit and restoration

Permit steps, backfill and surface repair add to the work.
