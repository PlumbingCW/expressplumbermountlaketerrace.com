Which service do I need?

Most calls start with a symptom, not a service name. Slow sinks and gurgling lines point toward drain cleaning. A lukewarm shower or a puddle by the tank points toward water heater repair. A water bill that jumped with no change in habits points toward leak detection. When a line has already split, burst pipe repair is the page to read.

Planned work follows its own path. A water heater at the end of its life moves to replacement, and a remodel or addition moves to plumbing installation. If you are unsure where your symptom belongs, call (206) 864-6558 and the dispatcher will sort it.

Emergency or planned?

Active water, a sewage backup, no hot water in cold weather and a flooded floor are emergencies, and the line is staffed 24/7 for them. A slow faucet, a quiet drip or a fixture upgrade can wait for a same-day or scheduled visit when available. The dispatcher helps you decide on the call.
