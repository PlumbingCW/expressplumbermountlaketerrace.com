A small drip is a slow water bill, and sometimes a flood in waiting

Most people treat a dripping faucet as a nuisance. It becomes urgent when water will not shut off, sprays from the stem, or leaks into a cabinet or wall. Our dispatchers answer 24/7, and while you wait for a plumber you can usually limit the damage yourself.

Look first for the small oval or round handle at the pipe under the sink, and turn it clockwise. If there is none or it will not move, close the main shutoff and open the lowest faucet in the house to drop the pressure.

A drip can also hide a cracked cartridge or a worn seat that is already failing. Old faucets seldom get better on their own.

Burst supply stop or spraying valve right now

A fixture that is spraying or a supply stop that has let go can empty a lot of water onto a floor in a short time. These steps come before anything else.

  • 1Close the shutoff valve for the fixture, or close the main water shutoff if the fixture has none.
  • 2Open a faucet at the lowest point of the house to drain pipe pressure.
  • 3Move rugs, boxes and electronics away from the water. If the water has reached an outlet or appliance, leave it alone.
  • 4Switch off the breaker only if the panel is dry and you can reach it without stepping in water.
  • 5Call (206) 864-6558, say which fixture is leaking and tell us if you have shut it off.

How we repair a faucet that keeps leaking

We shut off the water to the fixture, remove the handle and look at the cartridge, seat and O-rings. Compression faucets have a rubber washer on a stem. Cartridge and ball styles use a sealed insert that is replaced as a unit.

If the valve body itself is corroded or scored, a new cartridge will not seal. At that point we suggest replacing the faucet, and we explain why before touching anything else.

We then test every connection with the water on, including the supply lines and the stops, because a faucet job that leaves a weeping connection is not finished.

What changes the price of a faucet repair

What you can safely try, and what to avoid

A few quick checks are safe. Some moves turn a drip into a flood.

  • ✓Do turn the supply stop clockwise. Use your hand. If it will not move or starts to leak, stop and call.
  • ✓Do not force a stripped handle. You can break the stem and lose the ability to shut the water off.
  • ✓Do not wrap tape around a failing valve. Tape hides the problem and does not seal a worn stem.
  • ✓Do not leave the tub running while you wait. A tub that will not shut off belongs on the main shutoff until it is fixed.

Hose bibs, outdoor spigots and utility sinks

An outdoor spigot should be shut off at its interior valve before winter if there is one, and the hose should come off. A hose left attached can trap water in the spigot and split it. If a spigot drips inside the wall when the hose is on, close the supply and call.

Utility sinks in garages and laundry rooms follow the same rules as kitchen faucets. We can also replace a leaking valve behind a washing machine. If you are adding fixtures during a remodel, see plumbing installation.
