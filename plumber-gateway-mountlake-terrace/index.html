Why a grease line is not a household drain

Kitchen drains at restaurants carry fats, starch and food solids. These cool and cake inside pipes, so a snake can punch a hole through the mass without removing it.

Hydro jetting pushes water at high pressure around the pipe wall and scours it. At home, keep grease out of the sink and let it harden in a can instead.

Stop and call right away if

  • ✓Sewage is rising. Stay out of the water, keep children and pets back and call.
  • ✓You smell gas. Leave the building and call the gas utility or 911 first.
  • ✓Water meets electrical. Do not touch wet outlets or panels.
  • ✓A water heater is leaking heavily. Shut off the cold supply and the power or gas to it.
