Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information we collect when you use this site or call our line, and what we do with it. Last updated October 30, 2026.

Call (206) 864-6558

Phone calls

This website has no forms. The main way to reach us is by calling (206) 864-6558. Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. When you call, we may collect your phone number, your name if you give it, your address and a description of the problem so we can send a plumber.

We use that information to dispatch service, to follow up on the visit and to improve how calls are handled. We do not sell it.

Email

If you email contact@expressplumbermountlaketerrace.com, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply.

Website data

Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type and pages requested, for security and reliability. This site loads fonts from Google Fonts and a map from Google Maps, and those services may receive your IP address when the page loads. We do not run advertising trackers on the pages.

Sharing

We share your details with the plumber assigned to your job, and with service providers who help us run the phone line. We may disclose information when the law requires it. We do not sell personal information.

Your choices

You can ask us what we have about you, ask us to correct it or ask us to delete it by calling (206) 864-6558 or emailing us. If you do not want calls recorded, tell the dispatcher at the start of the call and ask how to proceed.

Children

This site is for adults who manage a property. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
