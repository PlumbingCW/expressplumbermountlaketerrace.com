What a sewer camera sees inside the pipe

A sewer camera is a small lens on a flexible cable, pushed into the line through a cleanout or other access point. A light on the head shows the pipe wall, and the picture comes back to a screen so you and the technician can look at it together.

That view settles questions a cable cannot. A cable tells you something was in the way. A camera shows whether it was grease, roots, a cracked joint or a sagging section. Our line takes calls 24/7 if you want to talk through what you are seeing at home.

Inspecting versus locating

An inspection looks at the condition of the pipe. A locate finds where it runs, which matters when a remodel or an addition would cut near the line. A camera head can carry a small transmitter that a receiver above ground picks up, and that shows both position and depth. Tell the dispatcher if location is part of your project.
