About Mountlake Terrace Express Plumbing

We are a plumbing dispatch line for Mountlake Terrace and the towns around it. You call, a person answers, and we send a licensed and insured plumber to your door.

Call (206) 864-6558

What we do

Mountlake Terrace Express Plumbing exists to shorten the gap between a plumbing problem and a plumber at the door. The line at (206) 864-6558 is staffed 24/7, including nights, weekends and holidays. A dispatcher listens to what you see, asks a few plain questions, and matches you with a local plumber who handles that kind of work.

We handle the everyday list that fills a plumber's day in a Snohomish County home: clogged drains, water heater trouble, hidden leaks, toilets that run or overflow and burst pipes. The full list is on the plumbing services page.

What you can count on

These are the only promises we make, and each one is something you can hold us to.

  • ✓A person answers. The emergency line is open 24/7, every day of the year.
  • ✓Licensed and insured. The plumbers we send work under a license and carry insurance. Washington lets you check any contractor on the state Labor and Industries Verify tool before work starts, and we encourage you to.
  • ✓A clear price before work starts. You hear the number, and what it covers, before a wrench turns. If the job changes once a pipe is opened, you hear that before the price does.
  • ✓Same-day appointments when available. Many calls get a same-day visit. We say when the schedule is full instead of promising a window we cannot keep.

What we do not promise

We do not promise an arrival time. Traffic on I-5, weather and a full schedule all move the clock, and a made-up number helps nobody who is standing in water. We also do not quote prices on this site, because a pipe behind a wall rarely matches a price list. The dispatcher gives you a real number once the job is understood.

We do not post made-up reviews, star ratings or customer counts. When you want proof of a contractor, the right place to look is the official registration record, not a banner on a website.

Why a dispatch line instead of a one-truck shop

A single truck can only be in one place. A dispatch line lets us match the job to a plumber who is free, nearby and set up for that kind of work, whether the call is a stuck toilet at midnight or a water heater swap next week. The dispatcher stays on your side of the call: they ask the questions a plumber would ask, so the person who arrives is not starting from zero.

Where we are

Our address is 22003 66th Ave W, Mountlake Terrace, WA 98043, near the 220th Street SW corner of town. From there we reach homes and small businesses across the city, in Lynnwood to the south, and in Edmonds, Brier, Kenmore, Bothell, Lake Forest Park, Shoreline and Mill Creek. The service area page has the details.

Mountlake Terrace grew fast after 1949, when the first compact cinderblock houses went up on a former landing field, so a lot of our calls involve older drain lines, original fixtures and water heaters that have outlived their tanks. Knowing that housing pattern helps the plumber arrive with the right parts and the right questions.
