Plumbing Installation in Mountlake Terrace, WA

Adding a shower, moving a sink or hooking up a washer is easiest when you plan the pipes before the walls are closed. We handle plumbing installation in Mountlake Terrace, WA, from remodel fixtures to rough-in, with a clear price before work starts.

Install now or pay to open the wall later

Every plumbing install is cheaper to do while the walls and floor are open. After drywall, tile and cabinets go in, a missed valve or a wrong drain location means cutting and patching. That is the main reason to call a plumber early in a remodel.

We cover new showers and tubs, kitchen and bath sinks, washer and dryer hookups, outdoor hose bibs and rough-in for additions. If you are not sure where the pipe should go, describe the room and we will go through it with you.

From plan to a working fixture

Most installs follow the same path, whether the room is new or old.

01

Walk the space

We look at where the supply, drain and vent sit now and where the fixture needs to land.

02

Price it clearly

You get a clear price before work starts, and we note anything that could change it, such as rot or a missing vent.

03

Fixtures go on, supply lines and traps are connected, and every joint is tested under water pressure.

We run the fixture, check for slow drains or weeping joints and show you the shutoffs.

What changes the price of a plumbing installation

Installs vary because every wall and floor is different. Here is what moves the number.

Distance to existing pipes

A fixture beside the current drain is simpler than one across the house.

Access

Open walls and a crawlspace make rough-in easy, while finished walls mean cutting and patching.

Fixtures supplied

A fixture you buy needs a good fit with the rough-in, and a specialty valve adds cost.

Venting and slope

A drain must fall at a steady slope and be vented, which can decide where a fixture may go.

Opening walls is the time to fix old traps

Older drains sometimes carry an S-trap under a sink, which can siphon its water seal and let sewer gas into the room. A P-trap tied into a vent holds its seal and drains smoothly.

If a sink gurgles or a tub drains slowly after an earlier remodel, the vent or trap may be wrong. We can check that before you add new fixtures to the same line, because a drain that already struggles will struggle more with another fixture.

If you hit a blockage during a remodel, drain cleaning clears the line before we connect anything new.
