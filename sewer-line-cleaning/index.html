Backed up main line: is it your pipe or the city's?

A sewer backup is an emergency, and our line is open 24/7. While you wait, stop all water use and keep the family out of the affected room. That much stops the problem from growing.

Your house drains into a lateral, a pipe that runs from the foundation toward the street. A blockage inside the house or in that private line is ours to clear. The main sewer in the street is the city's system. Several toilets failing at once, gurgling when another fixture drains, or water in a floor drain are the usual signs of a main-line problem.

Sewage rising: what to do right now

Dirty water in the house carries bacteria, so treat it with caution.

  • 1Stop using every fixture. Do not flush, run the sink, wash clothes or start the dishwasher.
  • 2Keep children, pets and bare feet out of any standing water.
  • 3If water is near outlets, appliances or a panel, do not touch them. Turn a breaker off only if the floor around the panel is dry and you can do it safely.
  • 4Skip chemical drain cleaners. They will not clear a main line and they make it hazardous to work on.
  • 5Call (206) 864-6558, describe which drains are backing up and where the cleanout is.

Public Works, the county line and the wet season

For a water or sewer line break in the city system, call Mountlake Terrace Public Works at 425-670-8264, open 7 a.m. to 3:30 p.m., Monday to Friday. After hours, the Snohomish County non-emergency line is 425-407-3999. We can clear and diagnose your private line, but a break out in the street is not ours to fix, so make that call if you see it.

Wet weather matters too. When the ground is soaked from fall to spring, a backup that appears alongside soggy yard patches near the lateral can mean a leak or a break rather than a plain clog. Tell the dispatcher if the yard looks wet, because that changes the plan.

How we clear a main sewer line

We start at the cleanout and send a cable with a cutting head down the line to break through the blockage. Roots are cut back, and grease and debris are pulled loose.

If the cable keeps stalling or the line coats back over quickly, we move to hydro jetting, which scours the pipe wall with high-pressure water. Afterward we run water through the line to confirm the flow. Where the cause is unclear, a camera on a flexible cable shows the pipe, and we can discuss a sewer inspection as a follow-up.

Early warnings before a full backup

The main line often warns you first. These signs mean it is time to call.

  • ✓Gurgling toilets. Air is being pushed back through the traps as water struggles to pass.
  • ✓Bubbles in the tub or shower when the washer drains. A shared line is slowing down.
  • ✓Water around a basement floor drain. The lowest opening is taking the overflow.
  • ✓Several fixtures draining slowly. One clog does not do this, but a partial main blockage does.
  • ✓A sewer smell that returns. Possible partial blockage or a dry trap.
  • ✓Wet patches near the cleanout or lateral. May mean the line is leaking, not just clogged.

What changes the price of sewer line cleaning

Main-line work is priced by what the line needs. These are the factors.

Length and depth of the lateral

A longer run takes more cable and more time.

Cause of the blockage

Grease and paper clear faster than roots, scale or a collapsed section.

Method

A cable is the simpler option. Jetting costs more but cleans the whole pipe wall.

Cleanout access

A good cleanout makes the job simple. Without one, we may need to pull a toilet or work from a vent.

Camera look and follow-up

Adding a look inside the pipe takes time but shows the cause.

Roots: why a main line clogs again

Roots find the tiny gaps at pipe joints and grow inside the line, because sewer water is a steady source of moisture and nutrients. A cable cuts them back, but they regrow unless the joint is sealed or the pipe is replaced.

If the blockage keeps coming back at the same distance, ask for a camera look. Depending on what it shows, the choice is between regular cleaning and a spot repair.

Living with a blocked main: families, timing and what comes next

A household of several people can fill a blocked line quickly, so the water has to stop. Use another bathroom elsewhere, such as at a neighbor's or a public restroom, until the line is cleared. Do not run the washer or take showers expecting the line to catch up.
