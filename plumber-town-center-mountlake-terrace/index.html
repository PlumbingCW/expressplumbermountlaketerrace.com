Why a Town Center leak is rarely just one unit's problem

The city describes Town Center as an 82-acre, transit-oriented mixed-use core with taller buildings near the station. Between 220th and 236th Streets, buildings stack apartments, shops and offices over shared plumbing.

In a stacked building, one drain stack or supply riser serves many floors. A backup in a ground-floor unit can start two floors up. When you call (206) 864-6558, name the building, your floor and what you see so the dispatcher can tell which line to trace.

Light rail, new buildings and plumbing that gets used hard

Link light rail service reached Mountlake Terrace in August 2024, and the Transit Center bus hub opened in 2009. Shops, cafes and restaurants in a busy area put their plumbing to work all day.

Heavy use shows up as grease in kitchen lines, supply stops that stick and toilets that run. If you manage such a space, a short maintenance visit costs less than an overflow. See plumbing installation for fixture work in a tenant build-out.
